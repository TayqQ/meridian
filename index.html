01 — Anatomy

Engineered
in layers.

  1. Sapphire crystal

    Domed 2.1mm sapphire, anti-reflective on both faces, so the dial reads like there's no glass at all.

  2. Polished bezel

    Turned from a single billet, then mirror-polished by hand for six hours.

  3. Sword hands

    Diamond-cut and polished. The lacquered seconds hand sweeps eight times a second.

  4. Sunburst dial

    Brass base, sunray brushed, eleven coats of clear lacquer. Applied indices, set by hand.

  5. Calibre M-01

    In-house automatic movement. 31 jewels, 28,800 vph, 72-hour power reserve.

  6. Exhibition caseback

    A second sapphire window, because the best part of the watch shouldn't be hidden.
